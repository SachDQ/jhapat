Play without YouTube’s ads

Jhapat plays the songs through YouTube, and YouTube sometimes plays an ad before one, which spoils the drop. Before the party, pick one of these:

💬

Open Jhapat from Messenger

The easiest: nothing to install

  1. Send jhapat.live in any Messenger chat: your party group, or a chat with yourself.
  2. Tap the link in that chat. Jhapat opens inside Messenger.
  3. Play from there. Messenger’s own browser doesn’t show YouTube’s ads.
🛡️

Or use a browser that blocks ads

Free, from the App Store or Play Store. Then open jhapat.live in it.

  • DuckDuckGoiPhone · Android · laptopBlocks YouTube’s ads on its own, on YouTube and in videos on other sites like this one. On Android, if an ad still shows, turn it on: Settings › Ad Blocking › YouTube.
  • BraveiPhone · Android · laptopIts Shields block YouTube’s ads. If one slips through, update the app: its block lists catch up with YouTube.
  • Firefox with uBlock OriginAndroid · laptopInstall Firefox, then add uBlock Origin from its add-ons.
  • Safari with uBlock Origin LiteiPhone · iPad · MacFree in the App Store; turn it on under Extensions in Safari’s settings. It doesn’t catch every YouTube ad.
These don’t stop YouTube’s ads

Private DNS and other DNS blockers (AdGuard DNS, NextDNS), Firefox’s own ad blocker on iPhone, and the blockers made for Samsung Internet. YouTube sends its ads from the same servers as the songs, so a blocker that only sees addresses can’t tell them apart.

Have YouTube Premium?

Then songs play without ads when you’re signed in to YouTube in the same browser: not in a private tab, and with YouTube’s cookies allowed.

Ads are how artists get paid on YouTube. When a song becomes your party’s favourite, find it on YouTube and give the artist a like or a follow.

Something on the table for every word

Each item is a word sung in the songs. Put out its prop, turn the item on in Jhapat, and the game only drops into those words. The five marked Starter are on when a phone first opens Jhapat, so start with those. After them come Sachin’s other favourites, then the most sung and easiest to find at home.

No two items share a prop, and nothing here breaks, spills or burns when ten hands grab it.

Loading the items…

The words that count for an item are the ones sung in the songs: Jhapat also counts the usual other words for the same thing, like प्रेम and love for Maya.

Play on the TV, with phones as remotes

  1. Get Jhapat on the TV: a laptop plugged into the TV, a TV with a web browser, or a Chrome tab cast to it. Open jhapat.live.
  2. Tap 📺 TV at the top of the Now playing card. A code and a QR code come up.
  3. Point a phone’s camera at the QR code, or open jhapat.live/r and type the four letters. Any number of phones can join.
  4. The phone runs the night: Start and Next, Pause, Show the word, Hide, louder or quieter, the playlist and what’s on the table.

On the laptop’s keyboard: → next song, Space pause, S show the word, R replay, H hide.

The laptop or TV plays the songs, so its sound is what the room hears. If a song won’t start on the TV, click the video there once: some browsers need one click before they play sound.

The rules, in four lines

  1. Put a few things on the table (see Props) and turn them on in Jhapat.
  2. Press Start. A song drops in 7 to 10 seconds before one of their words is sung.
  3. Grab the item the moment you hear its word. First hand on it wins.
  4. Not sure which word it was? Press Show. Holding the phone? Tap Hide and play too.

House rules that help

  • Only a sung word counts, not one in the title or on the screen.
  • Grab before the word and you sit out the next song.
  • Fewer items than players makes it wilder: three things for five people works well.

Free, no sign-up

Jhapat counts drops, never who: no account, no ID for your phone and nothing you type is kept. Your table, playlists and drops stay on your phone. The counts it sends are anonymous totals, like how many parties played tonight.
