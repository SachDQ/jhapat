Jhapat (झपट): press kit

A free Nepali party game. Put a flower, a bottle of water and a heart on the table, play a Nepali song, and grab the item the moment its word is sung. It started as a round on TikTok.

The round that started it

The round that started it: @suzupah on TikTok

The story

At Nepali parties a game went round TikTok: a song plays, things sit on the table, and whoever grabs the item first when its word is sung wins the round. Jhapat turns that into a page anyone can open. It picks a random Nepali song and drops in 7 to 10 seconds before one of the words on the table, so the room only has to listen and grab.

  1. Put out a few things: a flower for phool, a bottle of water for paani, a heart for maya.
  2. Turn the same items on in Jhapat and press Start.
  3. When the word is sung, grab. The room referees.
